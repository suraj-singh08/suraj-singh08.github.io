Insurance and eligibility

  • Insurance verificationConfirm active coverage, plan type, and patient details before the visit.
  • Benefits breakdownRead coverage by category: preventive, basic, major, and orthodontic.
  • Deductibles and annual maximumsTrack what is met and what remains for the plan year.
  • Frequency limits and waiting periodsCheck how often a procedure is covered and when coverage begins.
  • Coordination of benefitsSort out primary and secondary plans and who pays first.
  • Pre-authorization and predeterminationGet payer approval or estimates for major treatment.
  • Plan types and networksWork with PPO, HMO, and indemnity plans, in and out of network.
  • Fee schedules and write-offsApply contracted rates, UCR, and downgrades or alternate benefits.
  • Plan creationSet up and maintain insurance plan details in the billing system.

Claims

  • Claim submissionSend accurate claims electronically or on paper with correct details.
  • CDT procedure codesMatch codes, tooth numbers, surfaces, and quadrants to the treatment.
  • Attachments and documentationInclude X-rays, perio charting, intraoral photos, and narratives.
  • Claim scrubbingCheck claims for errors before they go out to cut rejections.
  • Clearinghouse rejectionsRead rejection reports, fix errors, and resubmit quickly.
  • Timely filingWatch payer deadlines so no claim is lost to late filing.
  • Primary and secondary billingBill the second plan with the correct first-plan payment info.

Denials and appeals

  • Denial managementFind the root cause of each denial and decide the right fix.
  • Appeals and reconsiderationsWrite appeals with supporting records and track the outcome.
  • Missing information denialsGather and send the documents or details the payer asked for.
  • Bundling and downcoding issuesHandle claims where payers combine or reduce procedures.
  • Denial trend analysisSpot repeat denial reasons by payer or procedure and prevent them.

Payments and posting

  • Payment postingPost insurance and patient payments accurately and on time.
  • EOB and ERA readingUnderstand explanation of benefits and electronic remittance details.
  • Adjustments and write-offsApply contractual adjustments correctly.
  • Refunds and overpaymentsFind credits and return money to the right payer or patient.
  • Deposit reconciliationMatch posted payments to bank deposits and daily totals.

Accounts receivable and patients

  • A/R follow-upWork aging reports and chase unpaid claims by oldest first.
  • Payer calls and portalsCheck claim status and resolve issues by phone and online.
  • Patient balances and statementsCalculate patient portions and send clear statements.
  • Payment plans and collectionsSet up plans and follow up on overdue patient accounts.
  • Patient communicationExplain bills and insurance in plain language.

Leadership and quality

  • Team managementLead onsite and remote teams, assign work, and track progress.
  • Training and onboardingTeach new hires processes and give useful feedback.
  • Quality auditsReview work regularly, find error patterns, and coach the team.
  • Client coordinationBe the point of contact for dental offices with clear updates.
  • Performance trackingFollow targets such as accuracy, turnaround, and collections.
  • Process improvementChange workflows to cut repeat errors and rework.
  • HIPAA and privacyHandle patient information securely and follow privacy rules.
